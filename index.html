Chapter 01 · 2 minute read

Two brothers. One Moon landing.

I’m Descent, the lower stage of Eagle. My partner, Ascent, is the upper stage. We’re not real brothers, but in this story we work like a team of them.

Scene 1 · Meet the brothers

Different jobs, same mission

Eagle was made of two stages. I’m Descent, the bottom stage. I have the landing engine, four legs, and room for supplies. My partner Ascent is the top stage. The astronauts ride with Ascent. “Ready to land?” I ask. “Ready when you are,” Ascent replies. (That conversation is imagined!)

Scene 2 · July 20, 1969

Down to the Moon

My engine slows our fall. The Moon gets closer. Armstrong steers around some big rocks. My landing legs reach the ground at the Sea of Tranquility. We made it! Ascent and I have brought the crew safely down.

Scene 3 · The Moon visit

A place to explore

Armstrong and Aldrin climb down my ladder. They explore, collect Moon rocks, and set up science tools. A plaque is attached to one of my legs. Ascent waits with the astronauts while they work. I keep our place on the Moon.

Scene 4 · Liftoff

Ascent takes the crew up

It’s time to go. Ascent’s engine fires, and the top stage lifts off with the astronauts. I stay behind at Tranquility Base. “See you in orbit!” I imagine calling. Ascent carries the crew back to their spacecraft in lunar orbit.

Scene 5 · Our shared job

One landed. One lifted off.

Ascent and I were built for different parts of the same mission. I helped the crew land; Ascent carried them back to lunar orbit. The astronauts would continue their journey home from there. We did our jobs together.

Story or fact? Descent and Ascent are the names I use as characters. The real spacecraft parts had no thoughts or conversations, and they weren’t actually brothers. Their mission roles and events are based on NASA records.
My job was to land them. Their journey continued without me.— The descent stage · imagined voice
Where am I now?
Still on the Moon

I’m still at Tranquility Base. The astronauts rode away in Eagle’s top half.

Did you know? · 01

Eagle had two stages

The descent stage helped land on the Moon. The ascent stage carried the astronauts back to lunar orbit. In our story, Descent and Ascent are their character names.

Did you know? · 02

NASA spotted Eagle from space

A spacecraft orbiting the Moon took a picture of the Apollo 11 landing place. You can see the lander and the astronauts’ walking paths.

The astronauts stayed about a day
